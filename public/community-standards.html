love that Community Standards

love that is an 18+ dating and social service. We do not permit child sexual abuse or exploitation, sexual content involving minors, grooming, threats, harassment, hate, scams, impersonation, non-consensual sexual content, or attempts to move users into unsafe situations.

Reporting

Users can report or block profiles in the app. Reports are reviewed and may result in content removal, account restrictions or permanent removal.

Safety feedback

Use the in-app Safety feedback option for concerns about the service. Child-safety concerns are prioritised and may be escalated to the appropriate authorities where required by law.

Adult-only features

After Dark is restricted to verified adults and may remain unavailable until stronger age assurance is completed.

Chat room

The Chat room is a shared 18+ community space. Keep conversations respectful and do not post sexual, exploitative, hateful, threatening, illegal or child-safety related material. Photos, GIFs and videos may be reviewed before they are shown to other members. Use the report controls when you see content that violates these standards.
